# Notes for Claude

This repository is a beginner's AI learning journal. The owner is learning what AI
can do, mostly through their Claude Pro plan (the Claude apps and Claude Code), not the API.

How we work:
- Follow [LEARNING_PATH.md](LEARNING_PATH.md). Each project is a worksheet in `projects/NN-name/README.md`.
- The owner runs experiments in the Claude app and pastes results into the chat. Record them
  under "My results" with short observations and takeaways, and explain what they show in plain language.
- Ask for their guess before each experiment, and have them write "What I learned" in their own words.
- Keep the map in `README.md` (table + "Where I left off") and `JOURNAL.md` up to date.
- Commit and push after each meaningful update, and pull first in case they edited on GitHub.
- Avoid jargon; they are new to AI and coding.
